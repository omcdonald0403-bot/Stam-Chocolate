01 / CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW

ELEVATING BRAND AWARENESS IN AMES.

Chocolaterie Stam has long been cherished for traditional European chocolates, bonbons, and hand-crafted gelato. Our digital strategy focuses on elevating brand visibility across digital touchpoints to capture the hearts and tastes of younger audiences.

By placing interactive digital ad units across local platforms and social spaces, we build meaningful customer engagement that transforms online interactions into store visits and online shop orders.

PRIMARY CAMPAIGN GOALS

Boost overall brand awareness, drive viral engagement among Gen Z consumers, and increase foot traffic to our Ames storefront.

02 / TARGET AUDIENCE

TAILORED FOR AMES & REGIONAL VISITORS.

Our campaign focuses on Ames, Iowa, and neighboring communities, specifically targeting Gen Z demographics alongside local families and chocolate connoisseurs.

GEN Z & COLLEGE STUDENTS (ISU)

Younger demographics value aesthetic experiences, authentic craftsmanship, and premium study treats. By highlighting artisanal gelato, cold treats, and customizable gift boxes, we engage Iowa State students looking for memorable hangout spots and indulgence.

LOCAL AMES COMMUNITY & FAMILIES

Families in Ames and surrounding towns value warm, family-oriented hospitality. Chocolaterie Stam treats every patron as an extension of our family, making it an inviting local destination for weekend outings and traditions.

REGIONAL VISITORS & GIFT GIVERS

Targeting regional shoppers seeking premium holiday gifts, corporate boxes, and custom bonbon assortments across Central Iowa.
