The Symphony of Roasted Beans & Handcrafted Belgian Chocolates

The tradition of fine Dutch-Belgian chocolatier craft comes alive as crispy autumn leaves blanket our cobblestone courtyard. At Chocolaterie Stam, our passion extends beyond simply roasting exceptional coffee beans; we curate moments of indulgence where rich cocoa, fresh-baked praline, and carefully selected aromatics meet in perfect balance.

A Century of Dutch Excellence

Since establishing our first shop in 1913, Chocolaterie Stam has maintained an unwavering dedication to quality. We source cocoa from sustainable farms and roast our coffee beans in small batches to preserve the complexity of each origin, from bright Central American profiles to deep, chocolatey Indonesian roasts.

Whether you are shopping for a cozy autumn evening at home or looking for the ideal holiday gift for coffee lovers, our Harvest Collection brings warmth, luxury, and unforgettable flavor to every cup and every bite.
