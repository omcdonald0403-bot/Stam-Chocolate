advertisement

ad-ver-tise-ment / ˌæd vər ˈtaɪz mənt /
  1. A paid announcement, as of goods for sale, in newspapers or magazines, on radio or television, etc.
  2. A public notice, especially a paid one.
  3. The action of making generally known or calling to the attention of the public.
