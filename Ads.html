CHOCOLATERIE STAM HOME

Chocolaterie Stam · Ames, Iowa

Campaign Description

To increase brand awareness of Stam with younger generations (Gen Z). This ad campaign is designed for the Ames community and surrounding areas. Also, our hook for this ad campaign was the promotional offer itself on the ad, and the inital phrase found on the homepage "Love our chocolate? Meet your new favorite coffee!" The phrase in written using an enthusiastic tone with the Gen Z demographic in mind. Meet your new favorite coffee!"
